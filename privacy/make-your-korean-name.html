Privacy Policy

Make Your Korean Name · 한국어로 이름 만들기
PSH Development Co., Ltd. — Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Who we are

“Make Your Korean Name” is an app by PSH Development Co., Ltd. (Republic of Korea) for creating a Korean name, name cards in Hangul and Korean-style seals. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the app on Android.

2. Data that stays on your phone

  • Your name and the Korean name you choose are converted and stored only on your device. We do not send them to any server.
  • Your creations (cards, seals and photos with a seal) are kept in “My creations” inside the app, only on your phone.
  • Photos: if you pick a photo to put your seal on, it is processed on your phone. We do not upload it anywhere.
  • Available credits and usage preferences are stored on your device.

Your images leave your phone only if you choose to save them to your gallery or share them with another app (Instagram, WhatsApp, etc.); that app’s policy then applies. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads: a rewarded ad if you want more credits, and an ad when opening the app from the fourth launch on. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device’s advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, and interactions with ads.

4. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and to improve it — for example which screens are opened, how many cards or seals are created, which kind of name is chosen, whether an image is shared, or whether a rewarded ad is requested. Data such as an app instance ID, the advertising ID, device model, Android version, approximate country or region and these interactions are collected. We do not send your name, your photos or your images to Firebase.

5. Your consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before using data for personalized ads and statistics. You can change your choice at any time in Credits → Privacy and ads. You can also reset or turn off the advertising ID in your Android settings (Google → Ads).

More information: how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

6. What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask you to sign up, and we don’t ask for an email or password.
  • We don’t sell personal data.
  • We don’t access your contacts, microphone or precise location.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with its date.
